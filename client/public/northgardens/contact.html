Frequently Asked Questions

What differentiates Kashmiri Mamra almonds from standard almonds? ▼
Mamra almonds represent less than 4% of the world's almond supply. Unlike California almonds which are chemically pasteurized and hold only 25–30% oil, Kashmiri Mamra almonds possess over 50% natural oil density, non-GMO genetics, and a sweeter, richer herbal crunch.
How should I store my dry fruits upon arrival? ▼
Our harvests arrive in nitrogen-flushed golden tins with airtight pull-ring seals. Unopened, they remain pristine for 18 months. Once unsealed, store in a cool, dark larder or refrigerator crisper drawer to maintain aromatic oils.
Do you offer bespoke corporate and wedding gifting? ▼
Yes. We offer handcrafted seasoned walnut wood chests with custom brass monogramming, personalized wax-sealed parchment messages, and customized assortment combinations for high-profile weddings and corporate holiday reserves.
What is the shipping timeframe and threshold for free delivery? ▼
All orders exceeding $75 qualify automatically for Complimentary Royal Express Shipping (2–4 business days with diplomatic parcel tracking). Orders under $75 carry a flat $9.50 express courier fee.
Are your nuts chemically bleached or heat-treated? ▼
Never. We enforce a 0% synthetic chemical policy. Walnut shells and almond kernels retain their authentic sun-ripened earthen hues without chlorine, sulfur dioxide, or paraffin coatings.
