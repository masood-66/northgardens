✦ SACRED GROVES ✦

Grown in Glacial Valleys, Hand-Plucked at Dawn

In the remote reaches of the Kashmir Himalayas and the terraced hilltops of Herat, dry fruit cultivation is not an industry—it is a sacred craft passed down through four generations of grower families.

Our trees are watered solely by mountain springs carrying mineral silt from ancient glaciers. Unlike commercial monoculture plantations, our trees are interspersed with wild saffron crocus and lavender, deterring pests naturally without petroleum insecticides.

✦ THE GOLD STANDARD ✦

The Triple-Inspection Guarantee

90% of retail dry fruits undergo chemical steaming, machine sorting, or sulfur fumigation to extend shelf life artificially. At Northgardens, we refuse every shortcut.

Tier 1: Harvest Time Vibrational Shaking

Boughs are gently agitated onto cotton tarpaulins; no nuts touch the damp orchard floor.

Tier 2: Alpine Sun-Curing

Naturally dried under thin mountain air over 14 days, gently evaporating moisture while concentrating therapeutic fats.

Tier 3: Master Visual Calibration

Each kernel is hand-inspected for weight, uniform color, and natural moisture balance before nitrogen sealing.
