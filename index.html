✦ UNCOMPROMISING PURITY ✦

Ancient Grove Farming & The Triple-Inspection Seal

Unlike industrial commercial processors who polish almonds with paraffin and bleach walnut shells with sulfur fumes, Northgardens honors the centuries-old Kashmiri tradition of sun-drying and wild forest harvesting.

01. 100% Raw & Unbleached

Natural earthy hues and unbroken botanical oils as intended by nature.

02. High Altitude Harvest

Grown at 6,000+ feet altitude where cool glacial waters nourish rich soil minerals.

03. Hand-Shelled Precision

Gentle wooden mallets prevent micro-fractures in delicate nut meats.

04. Nitrogen Fresh Seals

Packed in oxygen-evacuated golden tins to prevent fatty acid oxidation.
